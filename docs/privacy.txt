GeoPrefix contribution notice — version 0.2, 2026-10-11

The homepage asks whether you agree to contribute before requesting browser location. Agreeing shares one rounded observation. A new visit or another deliberate request can contribute another point; GeoPrefix does not track movement in the background. If browser permission is denied or location is unavailable, our SDK falls back to an IP lookup and sends no location observation. Choosing Use IP only does not request browser location.

Coordinates are rounded to 0.1 degree (roughly an 11 km north/south grid) in the SDK and again on the server. The service observes the connection's public IP, derives an IPv4 /24 or IPv6 /48 block, and stores a keyed pseudonymous address digest. The observation database does not store full public IP addresses or precise coordinates. It stores rounded coordinates, network block, capture and receipt times, uncertainty including a rounding allowance, provider-reported accuracy if supplied, unverified source/provider labels, app identity, retention choice and validation flags.

New homepage contributions explicitly select history retention. Each distinct capture is kept privately until withdrawn, including repeated visits from the same address and observations from networks seen in multiple places. An exact retransmission of the same address/capture timestamp is treated as a duplicate. SDK/API integrations can select retention=history only after disclosing this continuing retention. Existing contributions and clients omitting this choice retain their original 30-day policy; hourly cleanup can add up to one hour while the service is running. Earlier consent is not silently extended.

The response includes an observation ID and private deletion receipt. The homepage stores receipts, rounded display coordinates and capture times in this browser so you can review and withdraw your own contributions. Save a copy before clearing browser storage. DELETE /api/observations/{id} with X-Contribution-Receipt removes the private observation and its associated evaluation. Future exports recompute the network summary. Previously downloaded CC0 releases cannot be recalled.

Public downloads contain coarse network summaries supported by at least five distinct public addresses across two days. Address digests do not prove distinct people, and rounding does not guarantee anonymity. Repeat captures stay in history, but each address has equal total weight across its unique location cells in the estimated center. Multiple observed locations increase the reported spread and uncertainty radius. Dispersed blocks are flagged and do not receive an unsupported country label. Individual observations, address digests, app identities, deletion receipts and exact capture times are not published.

Observed median/P90/maximum distances describe the training observations' dispersion. They are separate from held-out prediction errors. New addresses are assigned to a stable holdout split; an address's repeated visits keep the same assignment. Legacy launch samples retain their original per-observation split. Held-out errors are summarized per address per UTC day to reduce repeat-visit bias. Browser positions can use GPS, Wi-Fi, cellular or IP-based information and are not independently verified GPS ground truth.

Known VPN, relay, hosting and satellite observations are kept privately with their supplied connection-type flag but quarantined from public estimates and evaluation. The current release cannot detect all such connections automatically. Avoid contributing through a VPN or relay. Embedded app tokens and source/provider labels do not prove coordinates are authentic. Requests are not access-logged by the app; infrastructure operators must configure their logs consistently.

Host apps are responsible for accurately disclosing the recipient, purpose, retention and withdrawal process and obtaining appropriate agreement before sending observations. Do not enable collection in child-directed apps or sensitive contexts without an appropriate consent and data-handling design.

Paid-service interest forms store the organization, contact and selected service for that request. No payment provider is connected and no payments are processed.
