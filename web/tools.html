01

Separate the evidence

Evaluating an estimate against the same observations that created it can overstate accuracy. Independent results need held-out evidence.

02

Keep the radius

A map pin represents an area. Uncertainty grows for sparse observations, mobile networks, VPNs, and networks used in several places.

03

Show what is unknown

No observed accuracy means no accuracy claim. Global percentages need representative coverage, not just a successful local test.
