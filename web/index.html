How observations become a network estimate

We group rounded observations by IPv4 /24 (256 addresses) or IPv6 /48. A block needs observations from at least five distinct public addresses across two days before publication.

Every agreed capture stays in the private history until withdrawn. Repeat visits are retained, but each address has equal total weight in the estimated center. If a block appears in multiple places, its observed spread and uncertainty radius increase. Widely scattered blocks are labeled dispersed.

The download includes the number of observed location cells and median, 90th-percentile and maximum distances from the estimated center. These describe the observations; separate held-out measurements assess prediction error. Browser locations are not independently verified GPS fixes.

Share your location?

Help build the IP database with one observation.

If you agree, our SDK asks for browser location and sends coordinates rounded to a roughly 11 km grid. We keep the rounded point, network block and time in a private history until you withdraw it. Only network summaries are published as public-domain data.

Your full IP and precise coordinates are not stored. Deletion receipts are saved in this browser. Avoid contributing through a VPN or relay. Read the notice.
